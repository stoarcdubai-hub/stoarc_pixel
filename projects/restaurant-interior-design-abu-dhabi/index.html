Thai Restaurant Interior Design in Abu Dhabi, UAE

Location
Abu Dhabi, UAE
Type
Hospitality Interior
Services
Interior Design, Consultancy

Thai Restaurant is a hospitality interior in Abu Dhabi, UAE, within our interior design consultancy work.

The vibrant contemporary interior creates an immersive and memorable dining experience, combining bold colours, organic architectural forms, arched openings, lush planting and decorative feature lighting.

Custom seating, sculptural ceiling elements, patterned surfaces and layered textures, together with carefully planned dining zones and circulation, balance functionality, comfort and visual engagement.

StoArc's scope was interior design and consultancy.
