Home / Projects / Autonova Motors

Commercial Showroom Architecture and Parametric Facade Design

Type
Commercial Showroom
Services
Architectural Design, Consultancy

Autonova Motors is a commercial showroom envisioned as a contemporary automotive destination where architecture and brand identity come together through innovative facade exploration.

The project investigates two parametric facade options. The first uses a dynamic vertical fin system that forms a fluid, continuous architectural skin with movement, depth and a sculptural silhouette. The second is a more geometric and modular facade using repeating three-dimensional elements to generate texture, shading and visual rhythm.

Both options are integrated with expansive glazed showroom facades, keeping vehicles and interior activity visible as part of the building's expression. The design combines computational thinking, façade performance, natural light and contemporary materials.

StoArc's scope was architectural design and consultancy.
