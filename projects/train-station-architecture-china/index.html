Train Station Architecture and Urban Design in China

Location
China
Type
Infrastructure Architecture / Urban Design
Services
Architectural Design, Urban Design

Casa Sud Train Station is an infrastructure project in China, combining architecture and urban design.

The landmark railway station proposal explores a future-oriented transportation hub integrating high-speed mobility, urban connectivity and public realm development. The design focuses on dynamic architectural forms, efficient passenger circulation, and integration between infrastructure, landscape and the surrounding urban environment.

The client was Dongguan Guotie Poly Industrial Development Co., Ltd.

StoArc's scope was architectural design and urban design.
